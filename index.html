Intro

EDIFICE INFRACON is a Structural Engineering & Design Consultancy, established in 2017, specialized in planning, analysis, design and engineering of complex structural projects across commercial, residential and industrial sectors. Our approach combines advanced engineering practices, analytical precision and practical construction expertise to deliver structures that are safe, innovatiove, sustainable and economical.

Over the years, EDIFICE INFRACON has developed expertise in delivering structural engineering solutions not only across India, but also for international projects, with design experience extending to markets such as UAE and Ethiopia.

Our structural designs are developed in accordance with applicable Indian Standards, Eurocodes and internationally recognized engineering practices. Each structure is comprehensively evaluated for critical design parameters including seismic effects, wind loads, gravity loads, lateral forces, structural stability, serviceability and overall strength, ensuring that the building performs safely and reliably under its anticipated loading conditions..

Through Advanced Structural Analysis and Modelling, we undertake design verification, structural checks and performance evaluation to ensure that every element of the structure is adequately designed for strength, stability, durability and serviceability. Our engineering approach combines code-compliant design with practical constructability and optimized structural solutions, delivering structures that are safe, efficient and engineered for long-term performance.

Beyond structural engineering and design, EDIFICE INFRACON provides comprehensive Project Management Consultancy (PMC) services for complex and turnkey developments. Our multidisciplinary approach enables us to support projects from initial planning and design coordination through execution, quality control, technical coordination and project delivery—ensuring seamless integration between engineering, construction and project objectives.

Services

RESIDENTIAL PROJECTS

EDIFICE INFRACON provides comprehensive structural engineering and design solutions for residential developments of varying scales and complexity. Our expertise covers independent residences, luxury villas, apartment buildings, residential towers and large-scale housing developments.

We focus on developing structural systems that seamlessly integrate with architectural requirements while ensuring strength, stability, serviceability, durability and construction efficiency. Each project is carefully analysed for applicable gravity loads, wind effects, seismic forces and other critical design parameters, with structural systems optimized for safety and economy.

COMMERCIAL PROJECTS

Our commercial structural engineering capabilities extend across office buildings, retail complexes, shopping centres, hotels, institutional buildings, mixed-use developments and high-rise structures.

We undertake complete structural analysis and design with a focus on efficient structural grids, optimized member sizing, lateral stability, load management and constructability. Our engineering solutions are developed to accommodate complex architectural concepts, large column-free spaces, varying floor configurations and demanding functional requirements while maintaining structural performance and project economy.

INDUSTRIAL PROJECTS

EDIFICE INFRACON offers specialized structural engineering solutions for industrial and infrastructure developments, including PEB (Pre-Engineered Building) structures, industrial sheds, warehouses, factories, manufacturing facilities, workshops and large-span steel structures.

Our industrial design expertise includes the analysis and design of steel frames, portal frames, trusses, crane-supporting structures, mezzanine floors, platforms and other specialized industrial components. Designs are developed with particular attention to large spans, heavy equipment loads, wind forces, seismic effects, operational requirements, durability and ease of construction.

By combining advanced structural analysis with practical engineering considerations, we deliver industrial structures that are robust, economical, efficient and suited to demanding operational environments.
